Subscription Graveyard stores your email (for login), a salted hash of your password, the time zone your browser reports (so renewal warnings arrive on the right day), the canceled subscriptions you bury ("Graveyard"), the planned cancellations you track ("Last Rites"), the upcoming renewals you track ("Renewal Watchlist"), and rate-limit records keyed by your IP or email so nobody can brute-force your account.

Who we share with. Cloudflare hosts the app and database. Resend sends transactional emails (verification, password reset, renewal warnings). How long we keep it. Account + content data: as long as your account exists. Security data (rate-limit rows, failed logins): purged automatically when the window expires. There is no newsletter sign-up in this app and we do not send marketing email.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA). You may access, correct, export, or delete your data. Open Account settings at the bottom of the app: Export my data downloads a JSON copy, and Delete my account permanently deletes everything. For other requests email privacy@example.com.

Children. The Service isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email privacy@example.com and we will delete it.

How Subscription Graveyard works

The whole app in two minutes.

The big picture

You cancel a subscription. You bury it here. The app counts what it's saved you and keeps counting every day it stays dead. Canceling stops being a chore and starts being a score.

What each tab is for

  • Graveyard is everything you've canceled, one tombstone each.
  • Last Rites is the waiting room for subs you haven't canceled yet.
  • Stats is your monthly savings and your total.
  • Bury is the form that turns a canceled sub into a tombstone.
  • Renewal Alerts warns you before an active sub bills. That one's Pro.

Burying one

Name, monthly cost, category. That's all that's required. Set the real date you canceled and the savings count from then, not from today.

Nothing here touches your bank. You type in what you canceled yourself.

The two numbers

MonthlyWhat you've stopped paying every month.
Total savedEverything the graveyard has saved since each burial. Only grows.

Your rank comes from the monthly number: Ghost, then Mourner at $50, Gravedigger at $100, Undertaker at $200, Grim Reaper at $500.

Just starting?

  • Bury one subscription you already canceled. Anything from the past year still counts.
  • Put two into Last Rites, the ones you keep meaning to deal with.
  • Check Stats. The monthly number is usually what makes the rest happen.

Free and Pro

Free covers 5 burials and 5 Last Rites, with every counter, rank and stat. Pro, with no caps and renewal alerts, is on the hosted version at subscriptiongraveyard.app.
