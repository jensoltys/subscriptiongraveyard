Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Subscription Graveyard is built and operated by YOUR COMPANY NAME. We believe a savings tracker should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, get around by keyboard, rely on high-contrast modes, or otherwise depend on assistive technology. This statement explains where we stand, where we're working, and how to tell us about something we missed.

Our standard

We aim for Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA conformance across Subscription Graveyard. WCAG 2.1 AA is the level cited in most US, EU, and UK accessibility laws and the level courts use to evaluate website accessibility claims.

What this means in practice

  • Keyboard navigation: every interactive element (links, buttons, tabs, form fields) is reachable and operable using only a keyboard. Focus is always visible, and Escape closes open modals.
  • Color contrast: body text meets a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background. Large text and interactive controls meet at least 3:1.
  • Form labels: every input has a label associated with it programmatically, so screen readers announce the right field.
  • Semantic structure: headings are in order, landmarks are used, and page regions are identifiable.
  • Alt text: meaningful images carry descriptive alt attributes; decorative artwork (tombstone art, ambient critters) is hidden from screen readers with aria-hidden.
  • No timed traps: there are no session timeouts that boot users without warning, and no modal dialogs that capture focus without an escape.
  • Resize and zoom: the layout reflows cleanly up to 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling on mobile.
  • Color is not the only signal: savings figures are plain text alongside the graveyard art, errors include text (not just red borders), and required fields are labeled.

What we use to verify it

  • Automated checks with axe-core and Lighthouse on the public pages. (EDIT THIS LINE to say how often you run them.)
  • Manual keyboard-only navigation testing on signup, login, password reset, burying a subscription, and account deletion.
  • Spot-checks with macOS VoiceOver and NVDA on Windows for key flows.

Known limitations

We try to be transparent about gaps we know exist:

  • Ambient animations: occasional critters (a bat, a ghost, a raven) fly across the graveyard, and tombstones age visually over time. These are decorative, are hidden from assistive technology, and are disabled entirely when prefers-reduced-motion is set. If any animation creates a barrier for you, please tell us.
  • Graveyard art: the tombstone scene is decorative; every figure it represents (per-subscription savings, monthly and total tallies) is also available as plain text in the app.

Tell us if something is broken

If you hit a barrier, anything that prevents you from completing a task on Subscription Graveyard, please tell us. We treat accessibility reports the same way we treat security reports: as a priority, with a real human reply.

Email: support@example.com
Subject line: "Accessibility barrier: Subscription Graveyard"
What to include (whatever you have): the URL, the device + assistive technology you're using, what you were trying to do, and what happened.

We'll acknowledge within 2 business days and propose a fix or workaround within 10 business days.

Formal complaints
