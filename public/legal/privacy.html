This policy explains what data we collect when you use Subscription Graveyard (the "Service"), why we collect it, who we share it with, and what choices you have. Throughout this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to YOUR COMPANY NAME.

1. What we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password (100,000 iterations, per-user random salt, SHA-256). We never store your password in plain text and never see it again after you set it. We also store your selected currency, whether your email has been verified, and the time zone your browser reports (like America/Chicago). We use the time zone so renewal warnings go out on the right day where you live.
  • Graveyard data. The canceled subscriptions you bury (name, monthly cost, category, cancellation date, optional start date, optional reason). This is visible only to you when you are logged in.
  • Last Rites data. The subscriptions you are planning to cancel (name, monthly cost, category, optional notes).
  • Renewal Watchlist data (Pro). The upcoming renewals you ask us to warn you about (name, monthly cost, category, next renewal date, billing cycle, how many days ahead to warn you, whether the reminder is a cancel or a negotiate reminder, an optional cancel URL, and optional notes).
  • Security data. A bounded record of failed login attempts and rate-limit counters, keyed by a one-way hash of your IP address or by your email address, used solely to throttle abuse. Password-reset and email-verification tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes so a database snapshot leak cannot directly compromise a pending reset.
  • Security log. When someone logs in, fails to log in, changes or resets a password, or deletes an account, we write one line to our server log with the event type, your user ID or a one-way hash of your email, and a one-way hash of the IP address. It never holds your password, your login token, your raw email or your raw IP address.
  • Cross-app Pro status. If you upgrade to Pro on the App Hub, this Service receives a signed token from App Hub that confirms your Pro status and your email. We do not receive your Stripe payment details. Card numbers, CVV, and other payment data stay with Stripe and App Hub. (EDIT OR DELETE THIS if your copy has no Pro hub.)
  • Newsletter consent. Subscription Graveyard does not offer a newsletter sign-up. Your account record carries a newsletter-consent flag that stays off unless you explicitly opt in somewhere that we add one in the future.

We do not run analytics on your subscription data, and we do not read it for any purpose other than showing it back to you and sending the reminders you asked for.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account (legal basis: contract).
  • To store the burials, Last Rites entries, and watchlist items you ask us to store (legal basis: contract).
  • To send Pro renewal and negotiation reminder emails on the schedule you set (legal basis: contract).
  • To recognize you across connected apps when you have a Pro subscription (legal basis: contract).
  • To prevent brute-force login, credential stuffing, and abuse (legal basis: legitimate interest).
  • To send you transactional emails (email verification, password reset) (legal basis: contract).

3. Who we share it with (processors)

We use a small set of vendors to operate the Service. Each vendor only receives the minimum data they need.

  • Cloudflare hosts the Service and our database (Cloudflare Workers + D1).
  • Resend sends transactional email: email verification, password reset, and Pro renewal warnings.
  • The App Hub (only if this site connects one) issues a signed Pro token that this Service checks to turn on Pro features. When you log in, we send your email to App Hub to ask whether you have Pro, and when a saved Pro token runs out, we send it back to App Hub for a fresh one. App Hub uses Stripe for payments. Stripe never talks to Subscription Graveyard directly. (EDIT OR DELETE THIS if your copy has no Pro hub.)
  • Pro recap emails, through App Hub. If you have Pro and haven't turned recaps off, App Hub asks this Service once a month for a summary of your previous month: how many subscriptions you've buried, your monthly savings rate, what you saved that month, the names and monthly costs of up to 10 new burials, your Last Rites count, and the names, dates, and reminder types of up to 10 watchlist renewals coming up in the next 30 days. App Hub turns that into an email and sends it through Resend. You can turn recaps off in App Hub or with the unsubscribe link in any recap. (EDIT OR DELETE THIS if your copy has no Pro hub.)
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, if this site turns it on, counts page views. Cloudflare says it doesn't use cookies or local storage for this and doesn't fingerprint visitors.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which sends your IP address and browser details to Google when the page loads.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not transfer your data to third parties for their own marketing.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Account, burials, Last Rites, and watchlist items: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account from the footer, we delete every row tied to your user ID (users, subscriptions, Last Rites, renewal watchlist, reset tokens, email-verification tokens, and your failed-login record) immediately and irrevocably.
  • Security data (failed-login counters, rate-limit rows): purged automatically when they expire, which is a matter of minutes for rate-limit windows. A daily cleanup job also deletes any expired rows that are left, plus used or expired reset and verification tokens once they're 7 days past expiry.
  • Security log lines: kept by Cloudflare's Workers Logs for 3 to 7 days, depending on our Cloudflare plan, then deleted.

5. Cookies and local storage

To keep you logged in, we set one cookie named sg_session. It holds a signed login token, it's marked HttpOnly so scripts on the page can't read it, it's only sent to Subscription Graveyard over HTTPS, and it lasts 30 days or until you log out, whichever comes first. We don't keep your login in browser local storage anymore. If you have Pro, we keep a Pro entitlement token in your browser's local storage. We also store small interface flags in your browser (whether you have seen the onboarding overlay and the tour, and which savings milestones you have already been shown). If you try a sample burial before you have an account, the name and monthly price you type stay in your browser's local storage (sg_guest_sub). They aren't sent to us until you sign up or log in, and then they move into your account. Once the sample is more than 2 hours old it's thrown out instead of moved in, and logging out deletes it too. While you're still typing it, before you press Bury it, what you've typed is kept in this browser tab's session storage (sg_guest_draft) so going back from another page doesn't wipe it. That goes away when you close the tab. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or cross-site analytics.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Subscription Graveyard doesn't use advertising or tracking tools that follow you across other websites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends that signal. We don't let any third party collect information about your activity on Subscription Graveyard to track you across other sites over time.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you. Open Account settings in the footer and use Export my data to download every record tied to your account as JSON.
  • Correct inaccurate data (edit your burials, Last Rites entries, or watchlist items).
  • Delete your account and all your data (Account settings → Delete my account). You'll be asked for your current password. If you signed up through the App Hub and never set a password, you type DELETE instead and we check the App Hub sign-in on that device. Deletion is immediate and we can't undo it. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Hub Pro subscription. Cancel that in the App Hub.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For anything that requires our help (CCPA right-to-know, GDPR Article 15 statement, dispute over a deletion), email privacy@example.com from the address tied to your account. We respond within 30 days (45 days for California requests).

8. Security

We protect your data using TLS in transit (HSTS), salted PBKDF2 password hashing, parameterized SQL with per-user row filtering, signed JWT sessions with revocation, throttling after repeated failed logins, request body size caps, per-IP and per-account rate limiting, and a strict Content Security Policy. No system is perfectly secure, but if we detect a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law.
